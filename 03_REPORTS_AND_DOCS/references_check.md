# References Check

## ICML (PMLR) and NeurIPS Indexing
- **NeurIPS (Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems)** and **ICML (Proceedings of Machine Learning Research - PMLR)** are widely recognized premier venues in AI/ML, but they are technically conference proceedings, not IEEE journals. Scopus does index many PMLR and NeurIPS volumes, but often with delays.
- *Recommendation for Capstone/IEEE strict formatting:* If the university requires IEEE/Scopus-indexed journal papers only, you may need to replace ICML/NeurIPS references with recent journal surveys or IEEE journal extensions of those works.
